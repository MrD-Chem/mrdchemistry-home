Polyatomic Ions

Some ions are a team of atoms with one shared charge. Find out when they need parentheses.

9.Compounds with polyatomic ions

Use the pink polyatomic pieces with the blue ones. Make neutral compounds that contain at least one pink piece. Challenge: use 2 polyatomic ions! In a formula, parentheses show how many polyatomic ions there are only when you need 2 or more of them. Example: one Ca2+ and two NO3− make Ca(NO3)2.
