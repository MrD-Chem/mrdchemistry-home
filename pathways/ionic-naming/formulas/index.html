Writing Formulas

Snap ion pieces together until the charges cancel, then look for the pattern.

Why? Since prehistoric times, people have experimented with chemical processes to make better tools, pottery and weapons. In the Middle Ages, alchemists combined compounds searching for the philosopher’s stone and the elixir of life. As chemistry became a true science, chemists realized that all matter is made of atoms and that chemical processes just rearrange atoms. They needed a simple way to show this, so they created chemical formulas. (Introduction adapted from POGIL.)
